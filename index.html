Why boards choose AA

Savings that never cost quality.

Cost saving

We have negotiated many financial agreements with the banks on our clients’ behalf: service charges kept to a minimum, other accounts earning the best possible interest rates.

Benefit of scale

Trades and suppliers rely on AA for a large portion of their work. That scale earns substantial savings through continuing negotiations with large companies and every other trade.

Head-on with trades

A background in the building industry and close ties to leading engineering firms let us meet professionals and trades on complex projects, which greatly reduces the chance of overcharges.
