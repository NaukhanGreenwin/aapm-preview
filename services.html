Condominium services

Everything the building needs, handled.

We at AA Property Management & Associates provide, but are not limited to:

  • Building maintenance and repairs to meet the highest expectations of our clients
  • Inspection and repair of mechanical, electrical and physical building components
  • Preventative maintenance programs
  • Financial services: budgets, financial statements, banking, payables, receivables, bookkeeping and record keeping
  • Collection of common element fees
  • Applying and enforcing the Condominium Act, Employment Standards Act, Occupational Health and Safety Act, Labour Relations Act, Workplace Safety and Insurance Act, Human Rights Code and Ontario Building Code
  • Enforcing the Declaration and the corporation’s Rules and By-laws
  • Staff management

Financial statements

Easy to read. Easy to understand.

Proven monthly statements your board can actually use:

  • Simple-to-understand balance sheet
  • Income and expense statements with a detailed breakdown of operating expenditures
  • Trial balance
  • Listing of cash disbursements
  • Chart of investments
  • Bank reconciliation
  • Business banking statements
  • Listing of accrued liabilities
  • Accounts payable
  • Accounts receivable
  • General ledger
  • Pertinent reserve fund expenditures

Other financial services

Disciplined money management.

  • Comprehensive financial reporting to the Board of Directors
  • Short- and long-term reserve fund planning for major repairs and replacements of the common elements
  • Collection and deposit of maintenance fees
  • Collection of arrears and the legal process for delinquent accounts
  • Supplier cheques under a strict purchase-order and work-order system
  • Payroll administration with direct deposit through ADP Canada
  • Annual budget preparation
  • Banking agreements with BMO, Scotiabank, RBC and TD to earn the best possible interest rates
  • Pre-authorized payment of common element fees for owners who choose it
  • On-time preparation of the audited financial statements
  • Registration and discharge of liens under the Condominium Act
