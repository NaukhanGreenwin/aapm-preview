About AA

Successfully serving the Greater Toronto Area since 1986.

Our goal is to build lasting relationships by providing service beyond expectation to each and every client, owner and resident.

Our approach

Professional, honest and accountable.

We accept challenges with a positive attitude and the highest degree of professionalism, focusing on honesty, integrity, loyalty, understanding, communication, accountability and the welfare of all.

Today’s condominium lifestyle is not the one of twenty or thirty years ago. It has become progressively more demanding and complex, and the demand for condominium professionals keeps growing. AA Property Management & Associates stands beside the Board of Directors with one common goal: to manage the assets of the condominium properly and professionally.

It is our policy to control our growth. We do not contract new corporations unless we have the capacity to manage them properly and professionally.

Credentials

Certified, audited, connected.

ACMO 2000 certified

A certification given only after intensive auditing and investigation by the staff of the Business Development Bank of Canada (ISO 9000). We were the 22nd company in Ontario to attain it.

ACMO and CCI member

A member of the Association of Condominium Managers of Ontario and the Canadian Condominium Institute for over 25 years.

Designated managers

A staff of managers and administrators, many of whom carry the ACMO RCM designation.

Cost saving

Scale that works for your budget.

AA Property Management has negotiated many financial agreements for its clients with the banks. Bank service charges are kept to a minimum, while other accounts earn the best possible interest rates.

Because many companies rely on AA for a large portion of their work, we gain the benefit of scale, and that has proven invaluable to our clients. We offer substantial savings through continuing negotiations with large companies and all other trades.

Relationships

Long-term suppliers. Faster, better service.

We pride ourselves on close, long-term relationships with our suppliers, which lets us deliver outstanding service response and quality.

With AA’s background in the building industry and its association with leading engineering companies, we meet professionals and trades head-on across a wide variety of high-level and complicated projects. This greatly reduces the chance of overcharges and leads to cost-effective control.

AA brings a unique, client-based level of service to owners and residents. We are always available for our clients, whether the needs are contracted or personally based.
